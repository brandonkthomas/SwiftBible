## Privacy Policy

**Effective November 18, 2026**

SwiftBible is a free Bible reader for iPhone and iPad. I do not operate a server for the app, and I do not collect, sell, or share your personal information. The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics, or tracking.

### Bible text requests

To show Bible text, the app connects directly to the provider of the translation you are reading:

- [YouVersion](https://www.bible.com/privacy)
- [Tyndale House Publishers](https://www.tyndale.com/privacy)
- [Crossway](https://www.crossway.org/privacy/)

These requests include what is needed to return the text, such as the translation, passage, and language, along with an app key that identifies SwiftBible rather than you. Like any internet service, a provider can also receive your IP address and basic connection information. Your highlights, notes, and tags are not included. Each provider's privacy policy applies to the information it receives.

### Information stored on your device

The app stores your personal highlights, notes, tags, reading position, and settings on your device. Your device backups may include some of this information, depending on your settings.

### iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud, these features are enabled by default:

- **Annotation sync** stores your highlights, notes, and tags in your private iCloud database.
- **Appearance sync** stores your theme, color scheme, and text size settings in your iCloud account.

This information is handled by Apple under its [privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). I cannot access it. You can turn off each sync option in the app's settings. Turning sync off does not remove information already stored in iCloud. Use your Apple Account's iCloud Settings to fully remove Bible data.

### Your choices and deletion

- **Delete All Annotations** in the app's settings erases your annotations. While annotation sync is on, this erases them from all of your devices and from iCloud.
- You can delete individual annotations in the app.
- Deleting the app removes its data from that device.
- You can manage information stored in iCloud through your Apple Account settings.

I do not hold a copy of this information, so I cannot access, export, or delete it for you.

### Information from Apple

Apple may provide me with App Store statistics and crash reports, generally in aggregated form or from people who chose to share them with developers. If you join the TestFlight beta, Apple may also give me your tester details, feedback, and crash reports. I use this information only to build and improve the app.

### Sharing and links

If you share a verse, the text goes to the app or person you choose through the iOS share sheet. Links to outside websites are governed by those websites' policies.

### Children

The app is not designed to collect information from anyone, including children.

### Changes

If the app's data practices change, I will update this policy and its effective date.
